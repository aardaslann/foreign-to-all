# Title artwork

`berlin-night.png` is the title-screen background for Foreign to All. The
original `art/world/scenes/title.png` was kept. The new image follows that
original title's palette and theme, with the architectural pixel detail of
`art/cutscene/intro/berlin-rooftops.png`.

The final title uses the preferred centered layout, original fallback
font and diamond ornaments, not the initially proposed left-aligned serif
layout. Text, controls, the actual alien sprite and subtle sky animation are
live Godot elements, not baked into the PNG. The image is also the subdued
background of the character creator. No music was added.
